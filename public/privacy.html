Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Genuine Draw collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers genuinedraw.in and every merchant address on it (like yourshop.genuinedraw.in).

1. Who is responsible

When you play a merchant's coupon, that merchant decides why your details are collected (for example, to give you your prize or send you offers). Genuine Draw stores and processes the details on the merchant's behalf, and uses some of them itself to keep the platform fair and secure, as described below.

2. What we collect

  • Customers: name and mobile number; optionally area/locality and birthday; whether you agreed to receive offers; the coupon you played and its result; the time, device type and IP address of the play. If you raise a complaint: what you write, any photo you add, and the replies.
  • Customer portal sign-in: your mobile number and a one-time code sent to you on WhatsApp. Codes are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Merchants and staff: business name, owner name, phone, email, address, GST number (optional), usernames, and a scrambled (hashed) version of passwords, which we never store in plain text.
  • Activity log: a tamper-evident record of important actions (coupon played, prize redeemed, settings changed). In this log, customer names and numbers are partly hidden.

3. Why we use it

  • To run campaigns: decide and record results, let the shop hand over prizes, and stop the same coupon being used twice.
  • To let anyone verify a coupon at genuinedraw.in/verify. Only the shop, campaign, result and a partly hidden name and number are shown.
  • To show you your own coupons and complaints at genuinedraw.in/my, and send you WhatsApp updates about them.
  • To handle complaints and keep the platform secure and fair.
  • For offers from the merchant, only if you ticked the box to receive them.

4. Who can see it

  • The merchant whose coupon you played, and their staff, can see the details you gave them.
  • The Genuine Draw team can see data when needed to support merchants, resolve complaints, or keep the service secure.
  • Service providers that host and run the platform (for example, cloud hosting, database and WhatsApp messaging providers) process data for us under their own security commitments.
  • We do not sell personal information. We share it with authorities only when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep campaign and complaint records for as long as the merchant's account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to settle disputes and meet legal obligations. The activity log is designed never to be edited, so entries in it are kept for as long as the log is.

6. Your choices

  • You can stop receiving offers by telling the merchant, or by contacting us.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by writing to us. Some records (such as the fact that a coupon was played) may need to be kept to protect against fraud and to settle disputes.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords and login codes are stored as one-way hashes. Each merchant can see only its own data, and important actions are recorded in a tamper-evident log.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies. The coupon page may remember on your phone that you played a coupon, so you can see your reward again.

9. Children

Genuine Draw is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before taking part.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
