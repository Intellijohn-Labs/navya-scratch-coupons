FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Do my customers need to install an app?

No. They scan the QR code with their phone camera and everything opens in the browser.

Can I use my own logo and colours?

Yes. Your logo, colours, address and terms appear on every printed coupon and every customer screen, and you get your own web address like yourshop.genuinedraw.com.

How do I stop people from using a coupon twice?

Every coupon has a unique code that locks the moment it's played. Reward codes can be redeemed only once, and your counter sees a clear "do not give again" warning if anyone tries.

Who decides what customers win?

For Instant Win you choose the gifts, their chances and stock. The result is picked by the server at the moment of scratching, and recorded in the tamper-proof log. For Lucky Draw, every coupon is one entry and winners are picked on the draw date.

Do I get my customers' details?

Yes. Customers enter their name and mobile number to play, and can share their area and birthday. You can download the list anytime.

What if a customer has a problem?

They raise a complaint from their phone. You reply from your admin. If it isn't sorted in 24 hours, the Genuine Draw team steps in to help.

Can I run it in more than one branch?

Yes. Add your outlets and staff logins, and see results for each branch separately.

How do I get started?

Register your business. Our team checks your details, usually within a day, and you can print your first coupons right after.
